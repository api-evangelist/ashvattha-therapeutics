# Ashvattha Therapeutics

Ashvattha Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company developing precision nanomedicines built on hydroxyl dendrimer therapeutics (HDTs), a platform exclusively licensed from Johns Hopkins University. Hydroxyl dendrimers traverse tissue barriers such as the blood-retinal and blood-brain barrier and are selectively taken up by activated cells in regions of inflammation, delivering known small-molecule drugs intracellularly to diseased tissue with sustained effect. Programs span ophthalmology, neurology, and inflammatory disease, led by migaldendranib (MGB), a subcutaneously administered nanomedicine in Phase 2 development for diabetic macular edema and neovascular age-related macular degeneration.
